Zide Fan is an **Associate Professor** and **Master's Supervisor** at the [Aerospace Information Research Institute (AIR)](https://english.aircas.cas.cn/), Chinese Academy of Sciences (CAS). He also serves as the **Deputy Director** of the Second Department at AIR, leading research in simulation and training technologies.

He received his Ph.D. in Cartography and Geographic Information Engineering from [Central South University](https://en.csu.edu.cn/) in 2016, under the supervision of Academician Jianya Gong. He then completed a postdoctoral fellowship in Big Spatial Data Mining at the [Institute of Geographic Sciences and Natural Resources Research](http://english.igsnrr.cas.cn/), CAS.

His research interests include **aerospace big data intelligent processing**, **intelligent modeling and simulation**, **remote sensing image understanding**, and **trajectory prediction**. Over the past five years, he has led more than 20 research projects, including the CAS Strategic Priority A-class Program, National Natural Science Foundation of China, and several major national defense programs. He has published 30+ SCI papers, authored 3 monographs, and holds 60+ granted invention patents.

He was selected for the CAS AIR "Future Star" Talent Program (2022) and serves as the Secretary-General of the Aerospace Big Data and AI Committee of the Chinese Command and Control Society. He is also a reviewer for IEEE Transactions on Geoscience and Remote Sensing, Computers Environment and Urban Systems, and several Chinese academic journals.

**Prospective Students:** The research group welcomes 2 master's students for the 2026 intake. Students with backgrounds in AI, remote sensing, or GIS are encouraged to apply. Strong programming skills are preferred. Please contact [fanzd@aircas.ac.cn](mailto:fanzd@aircas.ac.cn).

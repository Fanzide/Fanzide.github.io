Office 621, Kedian Building

Haidian District, Beijing 100019

China

Zide Fan (樊子德) is an Associate Professor and Master’s Supervisor at the Aerospace Information Research Institute (AIR), Chinese Academy of Sciences (CAS). He also serves as the Deputy Director of the Second Department at AIR, leading research in simulation and training technologies.

樊子德，中国科学院空天信息创新研究院副研究员、硕士研究生导师，兼任空天院二部副主任，主要负责仿真与训练技术研究。

He received his Ph.D. in Cartography and Geographic Information Engineering from Central South University in 2016, under the supervision of Academician Jianya Gong. He then completed a postdoctoral fellowship in Big Spatial Data Mining at the Institute of Geographic Sciences and Natural Resources Research, CAS.

2016年毕业于中南大学地图制图学与地理信息工程专业，获博士学位（导师：龚健雅院士），随后在中国科学院地理科学与资源研究所从事大数据空间数据挖掘博士后研究。

His research interests include aerospace big data intelligent processing, intelligent modeling and simulation, remote sensing image understanding, and trajectory prediction. Over the past five years, he has led more than 20 research projects, including the CAS Strategic Priority A-class Program, National Natural Science Foundation of China, and several major national defense programs. He has published 30+ SCI papers, authored 3 monographs, and holds 30+ granted invention patents.

研究方向包括空天大数据智能处理、智能建模与仿真、遥感图像理解、轨迹预测等。近五年主持科研项目20余项，包括中国科学院战略先导A类专项、国家自然科学基金、多项重大国防项目等。发表SCI论文30余篇，出版专著3部，获授权发明专利30余项。

He was selected for the CAS AIR “Future Star” Talent Program (2022) and serves as the Secretary-General of the Aerospace Big Data and AI Committee of the Chinese Command and Control Society. He is also a reviewer for IEEE Transactions on Geoscience and Remote Sensing, Computers Environment and Urban Systems, and several Chinese academic journals.

2022年入选空天院”未来之星”人才计划，担任中国指挥与控制学会空天大数据与人工智能专业委员会秘书长。同时担任IEEE Transactions on Geoscience and Remote Sensing、Computers Environment and Urban Systems等多个国内外学术期刊审稿人。
